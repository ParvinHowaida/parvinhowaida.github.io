Parvin Howaida

Writer & Digital Content Specialist

Writing has always been one of the great joys of my life. With a background in science and a deep love for discovery, I began my writing journey with science fiction short stories. During my bachelor’s and master’s studies, I also explored science communication through outreach and blogging, finding ways to make fascinating ideas more accessible. Over time, my interests even grew to include poetry.

Today, I work as a content writer and digital content specialist for a tech company, while continuing to write on different platforms and share my love of storytelling with others.

One of my proudest projects began during my bachelor’s degree: translating a book about astronomy for general readers from English into Persian. I got so much great feedback after the book was out there, available to Persian-language readers, especially young scholars.

Writing gives my life purpose and joy. Whenever I write, I feel more connected to the world and to the stories worth telling.
